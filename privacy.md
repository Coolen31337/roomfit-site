# Privacy Policy

Effective date: 26 October 2026

This policy explains what information the RoomFit app ("RoomFit") uses and why. RoomFit is provided by Nikita Iarchikhin, an individual developer based in Hong Kong ("we", "us"). Contact: roomfitsupport@gmail.com.

## 1. The short version

- RoomFit works on your iPhone. Your rooms, items, photos and gallery walls are stored **on your device**.
- We have **no accounts, no ads, no tracking and no analytics** in the app.
- The camera view used for AR is processed on your device and is **not recorded or sent** anywhere.
- If you choose to make a 3D model from a photo (when available), the cut-out image of the item is sent to our 3D partner to make the model — only after you agree in the app.

## 2. Information stored on your device

- **Camera and AR data.** RoomFit uses the camera and Apple's ARKit to understand your room (floors, walls) and place items. This is processed on your device. When you save a room, the AR map of the space and the positions of items are saved on your device so the room can be opened again.
- **Photos you add.** Photos you choose for pictures, gallery walls or items are copied into the app's storage on your device. RoomFit only accesses the photos you select.
- **Your items and settings**, such as sizes, frames, rooms and preferences.

This information stays on your device and is included in your device backups (for example, iCloud Backup) according to your Apple settings. Deleting an item, room or the app removes it from the app.

## 3. Information sent over the internet

- **Catalog pictures.** Paintings in the catalog are downloaded from The Metropolitan Museum of Art's public website. Like any website request, this shares your device's IP address with that website.
- **AI 3D models from your photos (when available).** When you choose to make a 3D model from a photo, we send the **cut-out image of the item** (not the rest of your photo) and technical settings to Meshy LLC, a 3D generation provider in the United States, which processes it to create the model. We do this only after you give permission in the app, and you can withdraw permission at any time in Settings; withdrawing does not affect models already made.
  - We do not send your name, email or location.
  - Under its terms, Meshy may use the image and the generated model to improve its services, including training its AI models. See Meshy's privacy policy.
  - Meshy deletes generated models from its servers within 3 days. We keep the model for you until you delete the item. We delete the uploaded cut-out image from our servers within 24 hours after the model is made.
  - Please do not include people, documents or other personal information in photos you use for 3D models. RoomFit does not send photos in which it detects a person.
- **Purchases (when available).** In-app purchases are processed by Apple. We receive confirmation of the purchase from Apple (not your payment details) to give you credits.

## 4. Why we use information

Only to provide the features you use: AR preview, saving rooms and items, gallery walls and shopping lists, and making 3D models you request. Where the law requires a legal basis, we rely on your consent (3D models from photos) and on providing the service you asked for.

## 5. Sharing

We do not sell or rent your information and do not share it for advertising. We share information only with the service providers described above, as needed to provide the feature, or if the law requires it.

## 6. International transfers

Images for 3D models are processed in the United States by Meshy. We use providers that protect information in line with applicable law.

## 7. Children

RoomFit is not directed to children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has sent us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

## 8. Your choices and rights

- You can delete items, rooms and photos in the app at any time, or delete the app.
- You can turn off AI 3D models in Settings.
- You can turn off camera or photo access in iOS Settings → RoomFit.
- Depending on where you live (for example, under Hong Kong's Personal Data (Privacy) Ordinance or the EU/UK GDPR), you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal data we hold. Because most data stays on your device, we usually hold none. To make a request, write to roomfitsupport@gmail.com.

## 9. Security

Data on your device is protected by iOS. Data sent to our providers is encrypted in transit.

## 10. Changes

We will update this policy if what RoomFit does with information changes, and show the new effective date. Important changes will be announced in the app.
